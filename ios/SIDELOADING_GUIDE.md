# How to Install YT Downloader on iOS (Sideloading Guide)

This guide explains how to install the YT Downloader iOS app on your iPhone or iPad without an Apple Developer account. The app is distributed as an unsigned IPA file that can be sideloaded using free tools.

## 📱 What is Sideloading?

Sideloading allows you to install apps on your iOS device without going through the App Store. This method uses your Apple ID to sign the app personally, giving you 7 days of app usage before needing to refresh the signature.

## 🛠️ Installation Methods

### Method 1: AltStore (Recommended)

**Requirements:**
- Mac or PC with iTunes installed
- iPhone/iPad with iOS 13.0+
- AltStore app on your device
- Lightning cable to connect device to computer

**Steps:**

1. **Install AltStore on your device:**
   - Visit [altstore.io](https://altstore.io/)
   - Download AltServer for your computer (Mac/Windows)
   - Follow the installation instructions on their website

2. **Download the YT Downloader IPA:**
   - Download the IPA file from our website
   - Save it to your computer or iPhone

3. **Install the app:**
   - Connect your iPhone/iPad to your computer with USB
   - Open AltStore on your iPhone
   - Tap the "+" icon in the top-left corner
   - Select the YT Downloader IPA file
   - Enter your Apple ID and password when prompted
   - Wait for installation to complete

4. **Trust the developer:**
   - Go to Settings → General → VPN & Device Management
   - Find your Apple ID in the list
   - Tap "Trust" and confirm

**Refreshing the app:**
- Every 7 days, you'll need to refresh the app signature
- Open AltStore and tap the refresh button next to YT Downloader
- Connect your device to your computer if needed

### Method 2: SideStore (Open Source Alternative)

**Requirements:**
- Mac or PC
- iPhone/iPad with iOS 14.0+
- SideStore app

**Steps:**

1. **Install SideStore:**
   - Visit [sidestore.io](https://sidestore.io/)
   - Download SideStore for your platform
   - Follow their setup guide

2. **Install YT Downloader:**
   - Similar process to AltStore
   - Use SideStore to sideload the IPA file
   - Enter your Apple ID credentials when prompted

3. **Refresh periodically:**
   - SideStore also requires refreshing every 7 days
   - Use the SideStore app to refresh the app signature

### Method 3: Sideloadly (Windows/Linux)

**Requirements:**
- Windows or Linux computer
- iPhone/iPad with iOS 13.0+
- Sideloadly software
- Lightning cable

**Steps:**

1. **Download Sideloadly:**
   - Visit [sideloadly.io](https://sideloadly.io/)
   - Download the version for your operating system

2. **Install Sideloadly:**
   - Extract and run the Sideloadly application
   - Connect your iPhone/iPad to your computer

3. **Sideload the app:**
   - Open Sideloadly on your computer
   - Select your device from the dropdown
   - Browse and select the YT Downloader IPA file
   - Enter your Apple ID and password
   - Click "Start" to begin installation

4. **Complete installation:**
   - Wait for the installation to complete
   - The app will appear on your home screen
   - Trust the developer in Settings if prompted

## ⚠️ Important Notes

### 7-Day Refresh Requirement
- All sideloaded apps need to be refreshed every 7 days
- This is an Apple limitation, not a limitation of our app
- Set a reminder to refresh the app before it expires
- The app will stop working if not refreshed in time

### Apple ID Security
- Your Apple ID credentials are used only for signing
- They are sent directly to Apple's servers
- Neither we nor the sideloading tools store your credentials
- You can use a separate Apple ID for sideloading if preferred

### App Limitations
- Sideloaded apps cannot use certain Apple services
- Background refresh may be limited
- Some features may behave differently than App Store apps
- This is normal for sideloaded applications

### Device Compatibility
- iOS 13.0 or later required
- Works on iPhone and iPad
- Not compatible with iPod touch or older devices
- Some features may vary by iOS version

## 🔧 Troubleshooting

### Installation Fails
- Ensure your device is connected properly
- Check that you have the latest version of the sideloading tool
- Try restarting both your computer and device
- Make sure iTunes is installed and updated (for AltStore)

### App Won't Open
- Go to Settings → General → VPN & Device Management
- Trust the developer certificate
- Restart your device
- Reinstall the app if problems persist

### Signature Expired
- Refresh the app using your sideloading tool
- Connect your device to your computer if required
- Enter your Apple ID credentials again
- The app will work for another 7 days

### "Untrusted Developer" Error
- Go to Settings → General → VPN & Device Management
- Find the developer profile
- Tap "Trust" and confirm
- This is normal for sideloaded apps
